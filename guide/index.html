Setup guide

Three steps, about 5 minutes, only once. Do them in this order so you do not have to sign in twice.

The account that creates the app in Spotify for Developers needs Spotify Premium: it is a Spotify rule for apps in Development Mode. Just want to look around? Try the demo, no account needed.

  1. Install Shuffl on your phone

    Open juandelmazovazquez.github.io/shuffl on your phone:

    • iPhone: open the app (the link above) in Safari, tap Share and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
    • Android: open the app (the link above) in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu and choose “Install app”.
    • Computer: on a computer you do not need to: carry on in the browser.

    Do not sign in in the browser yet. On iPhone, Safari and the installed app keep their data separately, so you would have to connect twice. Install it and carry on from the installed app.

  2. From the installed app, create your app in Spotify for Developers

    1. Open Spotify for Developers and sign in with your Spotify account. Open Spotify for Developers
    2. Tap “Create app” and fill in the form:
      • App name “Shuffl” or any name you like.
      • App description any text, for example “Shuffle my playlists”.
      • Website optional, you can leave it empty.
      • Redirect URIs paste exactly this address (with the trailing slash) and tap “Add”.
      • Which API/SDKs are you planning to use? tick “Web API”.
      • Tick the box to accept Spotify’s terms and tap “Save”.
    3. Spotify takes you to your app’s page and the Client ID is right there (32 letters and numbers): copy it. You do not need to open Settings.

  3. Paste the Client ID in the installed app and connect

    Go back to Shuffl, paste the Client ID and tap “Connect to Spotify”. Spotify asks for permission once and you come back to the app.

Will someone else use your app with their own account? On your app’s page in Spotify, open “User Management” and add their name and email (up to 5 people).

If something fails

  • “Invalid redirect URI” when connecting: the Redirect URI in your Spotify app is not identical to the one above (including the trailing slash). Copy it with the button. To change it later: your app’s page → Settings → Edit.
  • Error 403 when loading your playlists: your email is not in “User Management” (it must be your Spotify account email).
  • Error 403 on everything: the account that owns the app needs Spotify Premium.
  • “Invalid client”: the Client ID is not your app’s (it has 32 characters).
